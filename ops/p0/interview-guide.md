# P0 Interview Guide — 75 Wedge Interviews

**Founder-led, non-delegable.** 25 caregivers · 25 visa/green-card holders · 25 generalist household CFOs. 30 min each, recorded with consent. Goal: choose the wedge by evidence (G1) and harvest the copy register. **Rule: past behavior only — never "would you use…". The Mom Test applies: they will lie to be kind; behavior doesn't.**

## Question flow

**Opening (5 min) — the last incident**
1. "Tell me about the last time a piece of life admin went wrong or almost went wrong." (Let them tell the story. Note the *words they use* — that's the copy register.)
2. "What did it cost you — money, time, or sleep?"
3. "What changed afterward, if anything?" (A workaround built = real pain. Nothing changed = pain insufficient — note it.)

**Current system (8 min)**
4. "Walk me through what happens when an official-looking envelope or email arrives today."
5. "Where do important documents physically live right now? Show me if you're willing." (Screenshots of folder chaos = gold.)
6. "What deadlines are you tracking in your head *right now*?" (Count them. Then: "any you're worried you've forgotten?" — the anxiety probe.)
7. Segment-specific:
   - *Caregiver:* "How did the takeover of your parent's affairs happen? What do you still not have visibility into?"
   - *Visa:* "What happens if you miss a filing window? Walk me through the last renewal."
   - *CFO:* "What did your last attempt to organize this look like, and when did it stop working?"

**Spend & workaround evidence (7 min)**
8. "What do you currently pay for anything adjacent — storage, password manager, Rocket-Money-type apps, an accountant, a lawyer's reminder service?"
9. "Have you ever paid a late fee, lapsed a policy, lost a deposit, or missed a claim window? What did it cost?" (Sum these across interviews = the quantified-pain slide.)
10. "Who else in your life should be able to see this information? Who *shouldn't*?"

**WTP block (5 min) — only after pain is established**
11. Describe the concierge service in one sentence (human-powered radar + exact-next-step reminders). Then Van Westendorp-lite: "At what monthly price would this be so cheap you'd doubt it? / a bargain? / getting expensive? / too expensive to consider?"
12. The commitment test: "We're running this as a real service starting next week — free for 4 weeks, then $X [their 'bargain' number]. Want in?" **(Conversion to the concierge list is the strongest datum in the entire study.)**

**Close (5 min)**
13. "If this existed and worked, what would you tell your sister/friend it is?" (Their pitch > our pitch.)
14. Referral ask: "Who's the most organized *and* most drowning person you know?"

## Scoring (fill within 10 min of each call, 1–5 each)

`pain_severity` (story had real cost) · `pain_frequency` (monthly+ touchpoints) · `current_spend` (pays for adjacent solutions) · `workaround_effort` (built systems that failed) · `wtp_signal` (bargain price ≥ $8) · `commitment` (joined concierge = 5; referral given = +1 bonus) · `access` (reachable channel exists to find 1,000 more like them).

**Wedge decision rule (G1):** segment wins on (mean pain_severity × commitment rate), tie-broken by `access`. Written before data collection so the answer can't be argued with after.

## Copy harvest
Maintain a verbatim file per segment: their words for the pain, the relief, the fear. The landing pages (see landing-copy.md) get rewritten from this file in week 3 — our copy is a hypothesis; theirs is data.
